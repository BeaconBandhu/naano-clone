Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is Naano?+
Naano is a B2B LinkedIn creator marketplace: companies discover and book vetted creators for sponsored LinkedIn campaigns at a fixed price per post set by the creator.
How does Naano find the right creators?+
Naano matches creators to audience fit, vertical relevance, geography, language and the buyer profile behind the campaign.
Which networks do you support?+
The current experience is centered on LinkedIn, with selected creator distribution across other professional channels.
How does per-post pricing work?+
Each creator sets a fixed price per post that is shown before booking. The campaign can be booked as a single post or bundle.
How does attribution work?+
Tracked links connect creator posts to clicks, leads and downstream pipeline so teams can evaluate performance post-by-post.
Do you handle creator payouts?+
Yes. Naano handles creator payment workflows so teams do not have to manage invoices manually.
What is the difference between Free and Done for you?+
Self-serve gives you the infrastructure. Managed campaigns add strategy, creator sourcing, coordination and optimization.
Can I upgrade or cancel anytime?+
Yes. The self-serve model has no lock-in and can be scaled as your campaign needs change.
