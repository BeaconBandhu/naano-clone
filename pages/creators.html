FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you start earning.

What is Naano?+
Naano is the B2B LinkedIn creator marketplace: B2B brands book creators for sponsored LinkedIn posts at a fixed price per post that you set. Creators from about 1,000 to 500,000 followers use Naano to monetize their LinkedIn audience with deals from B2B brands they already know.
Is Naano free for creators?+
Yes. Naano is completely free for creators — there is no subscription and no fee taken from your earnings. You keep 100% of what you earn on every deal.
How much can I earn?+
Creators earn on average €500 per deal, with top deals reaching €1,500 per deal.
How and when do I get paid?+
You get paid within 24h of your post going live, securely via Stripe or bank transfer. No invoicing, no chasing.
Do I have to sign an exclusivity contract?+
No exclusivity, no minimum and no lock-in. You pick the deals you want and quit anytime while keeping everything you have earned.
What kind of brands are on Naano?+
B2B SaaS brands like Lemlist, Folk, Ringover and Gojiberry, plus 20+ more, with new deals every week.
Do I keep control of my content?+
Yes. You write and publish every post yourself, in your own voice. Your content and your LinkedIn account are always yours.
How do I join?+
Apply in about 2 minutes, no commitment. Once approved, you can start browsing deals and picking your first collaboration.
